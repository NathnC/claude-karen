# Templates

Skeletons. Replace every `<...>`; delete any line you can't fill truthfully.

## case.md

```markdown
---
title: "<short title>"
against: <trading name>
legal_name: <from Companies House>
status: open        # open | waiting | escalated | won | lost | dropped
opened: YYYY-MM-DD
merit: strong       # strong | arguable | weak | goodwill
wants: <the remedy>
paid: <amount, method>
refs: [<order no>, <case no>]
next_action: <what happens next>
next_deadline: YYYY-MM-DD
---

## Timeline
- YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM <fact>

## Evidence
- <file or location> - <what it shows>

## Contacts
| Who | Address | Source | Checked | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Law (verified)
- <Act> s<n>: "<short exact phrase>" - <url> (fetched YYYY-MM-DD)

## Ladder
| Rung | Opens | Status |
|---|---|---|

## Correspondence
- YYYY-MM-DD out/in - <summary> - <draft path or thread id>
```

## Level 1: polite / goodwill

```
Subject: <what> on <date>

Hello,

I <visited / ordered> on <date> (<ref>). <One or two sentences of fact.>

<The ask, e.g. "I'd appreciate it if you could look into this, and I'd welcome a <refund of the dish / voucher> as a gesture of goodwill.">

Thank you,
<name>
```

## Level 2: formal complaint

```
Subject: Complaint - order <no> - <item> - <your case ref if any>

Dear <team / name>,

I am writing to complain about <item/service> <ordered/delivered> on <date> (order <no>), and to ask for <remedy>.

What happened
- <date>: <fact>
- <date>: <fact>

My rights
Under section <n> of the <Act>, <short plain statement, with a short quote if useful>. <Second right if relevant.>

What I am asking for
<Specific remedy>. Please confirm by <date>.

If this is not resolved by then, I will <next step stated as fact>.

I attach <list>.

Yours sincerely,
<name>
<contact details>
```

## Level 3: final response request

As level 2, plus: "Please treat this as a formal complaint and send me your final response by
<date>." Correct any misstatement of the law in one sentence each, with the section. Copy the
executive office.

## Level 4: letter before claim

England and Wales. In Scotland, adapt for the simple procedure in the sheriff court; in
Northern Ireland, for the small claims court.

```
Subject: Letter before claim - <legal name> - <ref>

To: <legal name>, <registered office>

<Summary of the claim in two sentences: what, when, how much.>

<Timeline, the law, what was asked and what was refused.>

I claim £<amount>, made up of <breakdown>.

If I do not receive payment or a proposal I can accept by <date, at least 14 days>, I intend to start a claim in the County Court without further notice. I refer you to the Practice Direction on Pre-Action Conduct and Protocols, and in particular to the court's powers on costs and sanctions where it is not followed.

<Documents enclosed.>

<name, address>
```

## Chase

```
Subject: RE: <original subject>

Hello,

I wrote on <date> asking for <remedy> by <date>. I have not had a reply.

Please reply by <new date, about 7 days>. If I don't hear back, I will <next rung>.

Thank you,
<name>
```
