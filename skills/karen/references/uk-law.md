# UK law map

A map of where to look, not a source. **Fetch and verify every provision on
legislation.gov.uk before quoting it** (SKILL.md section 3). Thresholds, fees and rates
change; check them each time. England and Wales unless noted.

## Goods (Consumer Rights Act 2015, Part 1 Chapter 2)

| Provision | What it gives |
|---|---|
| s9 | Goods must be of satisfactory quality (incl. fitness for usual purposes, durability, free from minor defects). |
| s10 | Fit for a particular purpose made known to the trader. |
| s11 | Must match the description. |
| s19 | Remedies overview. s19(14)-(15): a fault appearing within 6 months of delivery is presumed present at delivery unless the trader proves otherwise. |
| s20-22 | Short-term right to reject: full refund, usually 30 days from delivery/ownership (s22(3)); shorter for perishables. |
| s23 | Repair or replacement, at the trader's cost, within a reasonable time and without significant inconvenience. The consumer chooses which (subject to proportionality). |
| s24 | Price reduction or final right to reject after one failed repair/replacement, or if it's impossible or causes significant inconvenience. s24(10): no deduction for use if rejected within 6 months of delivery (cars excepted, s24(11)). |
| s29 | Goods remain at the trader's risk until in the consumer's physical possession. |
| s31 | Terms can't exclude or restrict these rights. |

Food bought in a restaurant is goods (s9-11) supplied with a service (s49).

## Services (CRA 2015, Part 1 Chapter 4)

| Provision | What it gives |
|---|---|
| s49 | Service performed with reasonable care and skill. |
| s50 | What the trader said about the service is binding if the consumer relied on it. |
| s52 | Performed within a reasonable time (if no time agreed). |
| s54-56 | Remedies: repeat performance (s55) or price reduction (s56), up to a full refund. |
| s57 | Can't exclude s49 liability. |

## Digital content (CRA 2015, Part 1 Chapter 3)

s34-36 quality/purpose/description; s42-44 repair, replacement, price reduction; s46 damage
to device or other digital content.

## Buying online, by phone, off-premises

Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013
(SI 2013/3134): 14-day cancellation (regs 29-31), refund within 14 days (reg 34), services
started in the cancellation period (reg 36). Exceptions in reg 28 (personalised, perishable, sealed hygiene goods once unsealed).

## Paying by card or credit

- **Consumer Credit Act 1974 s75:** credit card (or regulated credit) purchase with a cash price over £100 and up to £30,000: the lender is jointly liable for breach or misrepresentation. Check the current limits.
- **Chargeback:** card-scheme rule, not law. Works for debit cards and under £100. Time limits are usually around 120 days from the transaction or from when the service should have been provided; confirm with the issuer.
- **PayPal / BNPL:** their own buyer-protection terms; check the provider's current policy.

## Unfair practices and terms

- Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, Part 4 Chapter 1 (unfair commercial practices; replaced the CPRs 2008 from 6 April 2025). Includes misleading actions/omissions and drip pricing. Check which consumer rights to redress apply and their commencement.
- CRA 2015 Part 2: unfair terms not binding (s62).

## Travel

- **Flights:** UK261 (retained Regulation (EC) 261/2004 as amended): delay of 3h+ at arrival, cancellation under 14 days' notice, denied boarding. Fixed compensation by distance (currently £220 / £350 / £520), care and assistance, extraordinary-circumstances defence. Check the current amounts.
- **Package holidays:** Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018.
- **Rail:** Delay Repay schemes; the National Rail Conditions of Travel; Rail Ombudsman.

## Neighbours and noise

- **Environmental Protection Act 1990 s79(1)(g):** noise from premises that is prejudicial to health or a nuisance is a statutory nuisance; the council must take reasonably practicable steps to investigate a complaint (s79(1)). s80: abatement notice. s82: a person aggrieved can complain directly to the magistrates' court.
- **Noise Act 1996:** optional council night-noise powers (11pm-7am).
- **Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014:** ASB case review ("Community Trigger"); thresholds set locally (check the council's published threshold).
- Keep a noise diary: date, start/end time, type, effect, recordings.

## Housing

- **Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 s11:** landlord's repairing obligations (structure, exterior, installations). s9A: fitness for human habitation (added by the Homes (Fitness for Human Habitation) Act 2018).
- Renters' Rights Act 2025: check commencement of each provision before relying on it.
- Damp and mould: council environmental health (Housing Act 2004 HHSRS), Housing Ombudsman for social landlords.

## Data

- **UK GDPR Art 15:** right of access (subject access request). Art 12(3): answer within one month, extendable by two further months for complex requests. Complaint to the ICO, normally after complaining to the organisation.

## Councils and public bodies

Use the body's own complaints procedure (usually two stages), then the ombudsman
(`escalation.md`). Statutory duties are what to cite: find the duty in the relevant Act.

## Going to court

- **Small claims track:** up to £10,000 in England and Wales (personal injury and housing disrepair lower). Money Claim Online. Practice Direction: Pre-Action Conduct (send a letter before claim, allow a reasonable time, typically 14 days).
- **Scotland:** simple procedure, up to £5,000. **Northern Ireland:** small claims court; check the current limit.
- **Limitation:** contract claims 6 years (Limitation Act 1980 s5, E&W); Scotland 5 years (Prescription and Limitation (Scotland) Act 1973).
- Court fees change; check the current fee table.
