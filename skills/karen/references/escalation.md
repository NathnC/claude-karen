# Escalation ladders (UK)

Each rung opens on a date: record it in `case.md`. Check each scheme's current rules and
time limits before telling the user a date.

## Generic ladder

1. Complaint through the published process (level 1-2).
2. Chase once on the deadline (short).
3. Formal complaint asking for a **final response** (level 3), copied to the executive office.
4. Out-of-court body: ombudsman or ADR scheme, if the sector has one.
5. Payment route: s75 claim or chargeback (can run alongside, never double-recover).
6. Letter before claim (level 4), then small claims.

## Sector routes

| Sector | Rung 4 (independent) | When it opens |
|---|---|---|
| Banks, insurers, credit, BNPL, s75 disputes | Financial Ombudsman Service | 8 weeks after complaint, or a final response (then 6 months to refer) |
| Energy | Energy Ombudsman | 8 weeks, or a deadlock letter |
| Phone, broadband, pay TV | Communications Ombudsman or CISAS (the provider says which) | 8 weeks, or a deadlock letter |
| Water | CCW (support), then WATRS | after the company's process |
| Rail | Rail Ombudsman | 40 working days, or a deadlock letter |
| Flights | the airline's approved ADR scheme, else the CAA | after the airline's response or 8 weeks |
| Councils, adult social care | Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman | after the council's complaints process; usually within 12 months |
| Social housing | Housing Ombudsman | after the landlord's process |
| NHS (England) | Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman | after the NHS body's process |
| Private parking | POPLA (BPA members) or IAS (IPC members) | after the operator rejects an appeal |
| Council parking | Traffic Penalty Tribunal (outside London) / London Tribunals | after a rejected formal representation |
| Retail, general traders | ADR only if the trader agrees; otherwise payment route or small claims | |
| Data protection | ICO | after the organisation's response or one month |
| Noise / statutory nuisance | council environmental health, then LGSCO for council failings; s82 magistrates' complaint; ASB case review | per the council's process |

## Executive escalation

A short, factual note to the CEO or executive office, with the case reference and the one
thing you want, often unsticks a case faster than any ombudsman. Use it at rung 3. Keep it to
half a page.
